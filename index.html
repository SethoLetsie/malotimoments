Plan Before You Go

A few simple tips make your trip to Lesotho safer and more enjoyable.

Mountain weather changes quickly, so bring warm layers, a rain jacket and sturdy shoes.

Main roads are easy to use, but some mountain routes are rough. Ask your host about road conditions first.

Greet people warmly and ask before taking photos of people or their homes.

Quick Facts

  • Capital: Maseru
  • Languages: Sesotho and English
  • Currency: Loti (plural Maloti)
  • Known as the Kingdom in the Sky
